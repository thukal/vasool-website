Most collection software looks the same in a sales deck. The differences that matter only show up after you've onboarded a hundred accounts and put the app in the hands of field agents. Use this checklist *before* you commit.

## 1. Who actually owns your data?

The single most important question. Ask directly:

- Can you **export** every borrower, loan, and transaction whenever you want?
- Is there a [self-hosted option](/self-hosted-loan-software) so the data lives on infrastructure you control?
- If you stop paying, do you keep your data?

If a vendor is vague here, treat it as a red flag.

## 2. Does it work offline — really?

"Offline support" often means "it won't crash without signal." That's not the same as **capturing collections offline and syncing later without losing a single entry**. Test it: put the phone in airplane mode, record ten collections, come back online, and confirm all ten arrive intact.

## 3. Can you prove field visits?

For any team doing field collections, you need [GPS and photo proof](/staff-tools) baked into every entry. Without it, you're trusting memory and honesty at scale — which never ends well.

## 4. Does it fit *your* loan types?

Generic tools force your business into their model. Make sure it handles the products you actually run:

1. Daily, weekly, and monthly finance
2. Interest-only / kandhu vatti
3. Gold and product-backed loans
4. EMI schedules and chit funds

See how flexible your options are on the [loan types](/loan-types) page.

## 5. Will your agents actually use it?

The best software is the one that gets used. Friction kills adoption. Look for:

- Fast entry (ideally [voice entry](/voice-entry-collection-app))
- Multi-language support for your workforce
- A simple, forgiving interface for non-technical agents

## 6. What does it really cost?

Watch for per-borrower fees, setup charges, and "enterprise" tiers that appear the moment you grow. Get the full picture on [pricing](/pricing) up front.

---

### The quick version

> Own your data. Prove your visits. Work offline. Fit your loan types. Get agents to actually use it. Know the real price.

If a tool clears all six, you're looking at software that will still serve you at ten times your current book. If it stumbles on any of them, keep looking.
