Ask anyone who runs an **agent chit** what the hardest part is, and they almost never say the money. They say the month. The notebook that only they can read. The WhatsApp group where the last four messages are stickers. And the evening before every auction, spent ringing twenty adults about a payment they already agreed to.

The chit itself is simple. Running it is not.

## What an agent chit actually is

A chit fund is a rotating savings group. A fixed number of members each contribute a fixed amount every month, for as many months as there are members. Each month the pool goes to one member — by auction, where the lowest bidder takes a discounted pot, or by draw. By the end of the term everyone has taken the pot once. It is a savings scheme and a loan at once, depending on whether you lift early or late.

An **agent chit** is the version run by a person rather than a company. The agent — the foreman — forms the group, vouches for the members, collects door to door, hands over the pot, and earns a commission on it. No branch office. The whole thing runs on that agent's reputation on that street.

You will hear it called different things depending on where you are standing:

| Region | Term |
|---|---|
| Kerala | Kuri |
| Tamil Nadu | Chitty |
| Andhra Pradesh, Telangana | Chit |
| North India | Committee fund |
| Globally | ROSCA |

Registered chit businesses in India operate under the **Chit Funds Act, 1982** and its state equivalents; agent chits sit anywhere from a ten-member family kuri to a full-time operation. Wherever yours sits, your records are what protect you when a member forgets, disputes, or disappears.

## Why an agent chit breaks past twenty members

Up to fifteen or twenty members, memory works. Past that, three things start to fail, in this order.

- **Telling everyone.** Every cycle, every member needs a reminder. That is not one message, it is twenty — and next month, twenty more.
- **Finding everyone.** New members join through referrals, from streets you do not know as well. "Near the temple" stops being an address.
- **Proving everything.** Who paid cash, on which date, and who is two cycles behind. Without proof it is your word against theirs.

This is the part that gives an agent their evenings back.

When it goes overdue, they get one too — with per-loan de-duplication, so nobody is pestered twice for the same due while a genuinely overdue member still hears from you.

Two details matter for an agent chit specifically:

- Reminders are **sent server-side**, through your own WhatsApp provider account — Meta, Interakt, Gupshup, WATI or AiSensy. Messages go out under your business identity, not from a shared number your members have never seen.

Reminders were always possible — you could type twenty messages. The difference is that reminding stops being something you have to remember to do.

This is the problem most chit software ignores, and the one that actually slows a round down.

When you enrol a member, your agent **captures the customer's location in the field** along with their documents. It is saved once on the member's profile, beside their KYC vault and their referee or guarantor link. You never ask for directions again.
