Most lenders don't switch apps because their old one broke. They switch because the app slowed their agents down, hid their data behind a paywall, or lost a day's collections the moment the signal dropped. If you're weighing the **Vasool app** against other loan collection apps, the differences that matter aren't in the feature list — they're in what happens on a real field route, at the end of a real month.

Here is where Vasool is genuinely different, and why it holds up where generic tools stumble.

## 1. Voice entry, not another cramped form

Most loan collection apps ask a field agent to stop, unlock the phone, tap through a form, and type an amount — in the sun, often one-handed, sometimes with a queue of borrowers waiting. So they don't. Payments get logged in the evening from memory, or not at all.

Vasool is built around [voice entry](/voice-entry-collection-app) in **six Indian languages plus English**. The agent speaks the amount and the app records it in seconds. That single change removes the friction that causes under-reporting — the gap between cash collected and cash recorded almost disappears.

> The fastest app is the one your agents actually use at the doorstep, not the one they catch up on at 9pm.

## 2. Offline-first, not "offline-ish"

Ask any vendor if their app works offline and they'll say yes. But "won't crash without signal" is not the same as **capturing every collection offline and syncing it later without losing a single entry**.

Vasool works [fully offline](/features) and syncs when connectivity returns. On rural routes and in weak-signal buildings, that's the difference between a complete day's book and a day full of holes. Test any app the same way: airplane mode, ten collections, back online — and count how many actually arrive.

## 3. Your data stays yours

This is the question generic apps hope you won't ask: if you stop paying, do you keep your borrowers, loans, and transaction history? With many tools, your data lives on their servers on their terms.

Vasool offers a [self-hosted option](/self-hosted-loan-software) so the data lives on infrastructure you control, and a [white-label build](/white-label-loan-app) if you want the app under your own brand. You should never be a hostage to your own ledger.

## 4. Built for Indian lending, not forced into a foreign model

Most loan management apps are built around a single, tidy EMI schedule. Real lending in India isn't tidy. Vasool handles the products you actually run — across [every loan type](/loan-types):

- **Daily, weekly, and monthly** finance
- **Interest-only / kandhu vatti** — see the dedicated [kandhu vatti app](/kandhu-vatti-app)
- **Gold and product-backed** loans
- **EMI schedules, chit funds, and savings schemes**

Generic tools force your business into their model. Vasool bends to yours.

## 5. Every field visit is provable

"I went, they weren't home" is impossible to check on paper — or in an app that only stores a number. Vasool bakes **GPS location, photo proof, and tamper-proof timestamps** into every entry through its [staff tools](/staff-tools), plus live route tracking for the manager.

The behaviour change is immediate: when agents know every entry carries a location and a timestamp, phantom visits drop to near zero. That's not a feature you notice in a demo — it's one you feel in next month's recovery numbers.

## 6. Honest pricing, no per-borrower trap

Plenty of apps look cheap until your book grows and the per-borrower fees, setup charges, and surprise "enterprise" tiers appear. Vasool's [pricing](/pricing) is built to still make sense at ten times your current book, not to punish you for growing.

Judge every option on six things: **fast entry, real offline support, data you own, loan types that fit, provable visits, and pricing that scales.** Vasool was built to clear all six — most generic apps stumble on at least three.
